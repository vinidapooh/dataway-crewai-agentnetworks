## Trending Keywords

*   **Generative AI for BI (GenAI + BI):** This is the single biggest trend. Users are moving past manual data preparation and visualization, looking for tools that can *generate* SQL queries, *write* entire reports from natural language prompts, and *explain* complex dashboards instantly.
*   **Data Observability and Governance:** As organizations adopt more data sources and AI, data quality becomes a massive pain point. Keywords around "data quality monitoring," "data drift," and "data lineage" are spiking as businesses struggle with unreliable data feeding the new AI capabilities.
*   **Composable Data Stack / Data Mesh:** The monolithic BI tool is being replaced by a highly interconnected "composable" stack where data assets are governed by domain experts (data products). This is moving beyond just the visualization tool and into the architecture supporting it.
*   **Real-time Data Streaming Analytics:** The demand is shifting rapidly from historical reporting (batch processing) to immediate insight. Looker and Mode are pushing capabilities for streaming and edge computing analytics, making low-latency data a critical search topic.
*   **Low-Code/No-Code for Analysts & Citizen Developers:** While enterprise tools are powerful, the user base is getting broader. Keywords focusing on democratizing data through accessible, low-code interfaces (minimizing the need for a dedicated professional data scientist) are highly relevant.
